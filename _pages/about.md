I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://www.unl.edu/), advised by
[Prof. Qiang Liu](https://cse.unl.edu/~qliu/). Previously, I received my M.S. from the University of California, Riverside.

My research focuses on **embodied intelligence**, with an emphasis on **edge computing** and **machine learning for robotics**.
I am particularly interested in how robots can manipulate objects and complete tasks by combining resource-constrained edge computing with server-side computation.
I am also interested in **multi-robot collaboration** and how robots can coordinate their actions to accomplish shared tasks.
